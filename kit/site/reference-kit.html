How to use this kit

Everything The Syndicate Club's landing page is built from, taken from their live site (thesyndicateclub.co.uk, 9 November 2026) and the Corn Cobs brief. Their brand as it is: their colours, their type style, their logos, their photos and film. Nothing new to decide. Load this page and the rules into Claude before changing the page.

Ingredients

Their colours, type, logos, ring mark, buttons, photography and film.

Compositions

Each section of the landing page as built, in their style.

Rules

Do, don't and why, plus the rules file for Claude.

Internal to Salo. The client sees the landing page and the concept review, not this kit.

01Ingredients

Colour

Their colours, read from their site's computed styles. Navy carries the brand; cream warms it; black frames the ring mark.

Type

Jost, as their site uses it (uploaded there as Jost Regular and Medium). Headings in capitals and bold, a light weight for the second line, as on their homepage. SIL Open Font License, self-hosted.

Logo files

Their own files from their site: white only, PNG, never redrawn or recoloured.

Ring mark

Their signature, used as their site uses it: small and centred above a heading on dark, and as a very faint watermark that turns as you scroll.

Buttons

Their style: square, capitals, widely tracked. A glint sweeps across on hover and the button leans towards the pointer.

Photography library

All 48 photographs on their site, as their own files. Course names are in the file names where their site gives them.

Film

Their homepage aerial film, cut to a ten-second loop: 685KB on desktop, 210KB on phones. Plays only on screen, with a pause button.

Partner logos

From their site footer. Not on the landing page: the brief keeps the footer to legal only.

02Compositions

Hero and enquiry form

Copy left 55%, form right 45%, over a shaded partner course. Their stacked logo top left, phone top right. On phones the form sits straight under the headline and subhead.

Course strip, review, one or eighty

Course names on their black, a review on cream, then the problem on navy with one dot against eighty.

Benefits and courses

Six benefits with drawn line icons. Their film behind the course heading, then six cards that flick through each course's photos.

Steps, fit and categories

Three steps that light up as the line reaches them. A navy yes panel and a grey no panel. Four photo cards, no prices.

FAQs and final call to action

An accordion on cream. Then the same form on a shaded course band, with the ring mark above and turning faintly behind.

Sticky bar

Appears once the hero has gone, hides at the final form, and shows reading progress along its edge.

05Rules

Rules sheet

Do, don't and why.

PieceDoDon'tWhy
BrandUse their colours, type style, logos, photos and film as they are.Introduce a new colour or style.It's their brand; there's nothing to decide.
CopyPaste the brief's copy as written.Rewrite it to fit the layout.Corn Cobs wrote it to match the ads.
Claims"Over 80 of Britain's finest private courses"."80+ Top 100".Their own FAQ contradicts it.
HeadingsCapitals and bold, a light second line where it helps.Sentence case or another font.That's how their site sets headings.
ButtonsSquare, capitals, tracked; navy or white.Rounded or coloured buttons.Their buttons are square outlines.
LogoWhite files on navy, black or a shaded photo.Redraw, recolour or put it on white.Only white files exist.
PhotosTheir partner courses, named in alt text.Stock golfers or carousels.Brief rule; named courses are the proof.
MotionTransform, opacity, clip-path and stroke, once, on screen.Anything that delays the form or loops without a pause.Speed affects cost per click.
