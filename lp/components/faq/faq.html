Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already be a member of a golf club?

No. There is no requirement to belong to a private club, and no need to resign from one if you do.

Do I need an official handicap?

Yes. Every member holds an official playing handicap. If you are in the process of getting one, tell us on the form, and we will pick it up on the call.

Can I play at weekends?

Yes. Weekend and bank holiday tee times are generally available from late morning onwards. Weekday play is open through the day.

Is the golf really free?

Yes, green fees are included at partner clubs. Members book one tee time a day, and a small number of the most sought-after clubs have extra limits at peak times.

How many guests can I bring?

Up to three, every time you play. Preferential guest rates typically run from £50 to £90 per person, with a handful of the most in-demand courses higher in peak summer.

How do I book a tee time?

Through your concierge, by phone, email or WhatsApp. Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm and Saturday 10am to 4pm, with most requests handled the same day. You never have to ring the club yourself.

What does membership cost?

It depends on which category you join. We will give you the full figures on your call, before you apply.

Do I need to be proposed by existing members?

Applications are normally proposed and seconded by two current members. We also consider applications without a proposer, so if you do not know anyone in the club, say so on the form and we will take it from there.
