Is this the right membership for you?

It works well if

  • You play twenty or more rounds a year
  • You want variety rather than one home course
  • You entertain clients, friends or family and want somewhere memorable to do it
  • You hold an official playing handicap
  • You travel around the UK for work or leisure

It is probably not for you if

  • You play a handful of times a year
  • You are looking for the cheapest possible way to get on a course
  • You do not yet hold an official handicap
  • You only ever play at weekends before 11 am
